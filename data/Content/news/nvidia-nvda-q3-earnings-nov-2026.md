## What happened

- Nvidia's fiscal quarterly report, released Nov 17, showed revenue up 103% year-over-year to $115.8 billion.
- Net income rose 105% year-over-year to $65.3 billion.
- Within days, the board-level drama around Jensen Huang's resignation and reinstatement took over the headlines, and the earnings beat barely registered in the broader coverage.
- The fundamentals behind the AI buildout -- chip demand, data-center spend -- didn't change because of a leadership story.

## Why it matters

It's worth separating the two narratives: the governance and legal overhang is real and unresolved, but the underlying demand numbers from this report were genuinely strong. Investors evaluating Nvidia should weigh both threads independently rather than letting one drown out the other.
