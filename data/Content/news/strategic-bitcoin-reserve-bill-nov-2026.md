Washington's crypto policy debate moved forward this week, even as bitcoin's price slid.

## What happened

- On Nov. 24, the **House Financial Services Committee** submitted the **American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026**.
- The bill would **codify a strategic bitcoin reserve** in law rather than leaving it to executive action.
- It proposes a **minimum 20-year holding period** for reserve bitcoin, aimed at preventing short-term selling.
- Separately, Switzerland's **BBIT spot bitcoin ETF** began trading, extending the range of regulated vehicles for crypto exposure in Europe.

## Why it matters

A legislated reserve would make the government a long-term holder and could reduce the risk of policy reversals. The bill still has to pass committee votes and both chambers of Congress, and its final form may differ substantially.

## What it doesn't change

Policy news rarely overrides market forces in the short run. The bill landed during bitcoin's steepest monthly decline in years. Investors should judge crypto on its role in their own portfolio, not on headlines.
