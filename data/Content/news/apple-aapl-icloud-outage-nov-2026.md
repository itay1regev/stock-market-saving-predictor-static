## What happened

- Apple's iCloud, App Store, and Apple Pay services went down on Nov 17 following an internal software update.
- The outage affected users broadly across these core services before being resolved.
- It landed in the same week as Apple's run toward a $5 trillion market cap, a reminder that scale doesn't eliminate operational risk.

## Why it matters

Outages like this rarely move the stock on their own, but they're worth tracking as a pattern -- services revenue (iCloud, App Store, Apple Pay) is an increasingly important slice of Apple's business, and reliability there matters to the thesis.
