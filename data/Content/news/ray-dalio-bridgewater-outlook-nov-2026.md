Ray Dalio doesn't love stocks or bonds right now — but he's still holding plenty of both.

- Bridgewater's $23B stock portfolio still keeps 16% in SPY and IVV, even after cutting both.
- Dalio's long-run expected return for the S&P 500: just 4.7% a year — below his ~5% estimate for long bonds.
- His stated concern: stretched valuations, high P/E multiples, tight credit spreads.
- He's hinting at a preference for non-US equities, bonds, and currencies as a dollar-decline hedge.
- Portfolio value: ~$22.99B as of Nov 22, 2026, still anchored by broad index funds.

Caution at the macro level, index-fund dependence at the portfolio level — a very Dalio combination.
