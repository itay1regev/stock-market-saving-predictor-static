- Pershing Square's Q3 2026 13F (filed Nov 16): $30.8B across just 16 positions.
- Top 5 holdings: Pershing Square Inc itself (33.5%), Uber (7.6%), Amazon (6.9%), Microsoft (6.8%), Meta (6.7%).

- Q3 results: net loss of $19.9M; revenue fell 14.5% to $56.6M.
- Nov 24: warns the US economy could tip into recession if the Fed holds firm on a 2% inflation target.

Concentrated bets and public warnings — Ackman's two signature moves, both on display this month.
