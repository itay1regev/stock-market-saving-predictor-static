Nvidia just had one of the wildest two-week stretches in its history — and it ended with the stock up over 30% in a day.

## What happened

- **Nov 20:** NVDA fell 14.0% to $125.53 after a Reuters report that the SEC is probing the company's accounting. The stock had already slumped more than 40% over the prior month.
- **Nov 23:** Nvidia's board unanimously voted to reinstate **Jensen Huang** as President and CEO, effective immediately — less than two weeks after he had resigned. NVDA closed at $165.05, up **31.5%** on the day.
- The rally recovered roughly **$952B** of the **$1.16T** in market value Nvidia had lost, and it dragged the broader AI trade back up with it — all three major U.S. indexes had just closed Friday in a correction.
- **Melius Research** upgraded NVDA to Buy with a $220 price target; **Bernstein** moved to Outperform with a $200 target.
- A sobering note: the stock gave back more than 5% the very next trading session, a reminder that this move was driven by a leadership decision, not a change in underlying fundamentals.

## Why it matters

Nvidia isn't just a chipmaker right now — it's the bellwether for the entire AI trade. When its CEO situation wobbles, it drags Big Tech with it; when it stabilizes, the relief rally is market-wide. Keep watching the SEC accounting probe — that overhang didn't disappear just because Huang came back.
