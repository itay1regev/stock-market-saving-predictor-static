## What happened

- Amazon announced on Nov 23 that it is acquiring AI startup Decart's chip-software technology and hiring its founding team.
- The deal is squarely about compute infrastructure -- software that sits closer to the chip layer -- rather than a consumer-facing AI product.
- It comes in the same month Amazon disclosed heavier AWS capital spending tied to its broader AI buildout, including its Anthropic relationship.

## Why it matters

Amazon keeps making small, infrastructure-layer acquisitions rather than one big headline deal -- a pattern worth watching alongside its AWS capex trajectory, since this is where the AI margin story will actually get decided.
