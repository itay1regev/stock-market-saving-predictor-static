US stocks just had one of their most turbulent stretches in years, and the epicenter was the AI trade that has led the market for so long.

## What happened

- The **S&P 500 fell for seven consecutive sessions**, its longest losing streak since the pandemic crash of 2020.
- The **Nasdaq 100 dropped nearly 5% in early trading** at one point and briefly fell into **bear-market territory**, 20% below its recent high. Its swings were the largest since April 2025.
- On Wednesday, Nov. 25, the **Dow erased its losses and closed up about 0.3%**, while the S&P 500 slipped about 0.2% and the Nasdaq recovered part of its drop.
- Hot inflation data and expectations of another Fed rate hike added to pressure on high-valuation tech names.

## Why it matters

Because a handful of mega-cap tech companies make up a large share of the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100, a sell-off in those names hits index investors directly, even those who think they own "the whole market." ## The long view

Seven-day losing streaks feel dramatic but have happened many times in market history. Investors with a long horizon and a written allocation usually do best by rebalancing, not reacting. See [Rebalancing Your Portfolio](#/learn/rebalancing-your-portfolio).
