## What happened

- TSMC reported October 2026 net revenue of approximately NT$586.89 billion, up 14.7% month-over-month and up 59.7% year-over-year, in a Form 6-K filed with the SEC on Nov 10.
- January-October 2026 cumulative revenue totaled NT$4,485.62 billion, up 43.3% versus the same period in 2025.
- This filing landed before the Nvidia CEO-reinstatement rally that later sent TSM shares up 14.08% on Nov 23 -- its best single-day gain since October 2008.
- The October numbers are a reminder that the rally had real demand underneath it, not just sentiment: TSMC manufactures most of Nvidia's advanced AI accelerator chips.

## Why it matters

A 14% single-day pop driven by a CEO headline is a sentiment move. A 59.7% year-over-year revenue jump filed with the SEC two weeks earlier is a fundamentals move. Both are true at once here, and it's worth being able to tell them apart when the next headline hits.
