## What happened

- A shareholder class action covering investors who bought INTC shares between July 23 and October 29, 2026 names CEO Lip-Bu Tan and CFO David Zinsner as defendants, alleging Intel failed to promptly disclose how falling PC sales and inventory buildup would hit revenue.
- INTC fell 7.0% to $55.47 on Nov 16, a drop tied partly to the suit and partly to a sector-wide AI-chip selloff ahead of Nvidia's earnings.
- Separately, Intel's foundry business posted a $1.85 billion operating loss last quarter, and the company has said it likely won't generate positive free cash flow next year.
- The stock has since been volatile with the broader chip sector -- up over 13% on Nov 23 amid the Huang-reinstatement rally, then down 8.1% on Nov 24 as that rally cooled.

## Why it matters

Intel's foundry turnaround was always going to be a multi-year, cash-intensive bet -- this is the market pricing in that it's taking longer and costing more than hoped, layered on top of a fresh legal question about what management knew and when. Both threads are separate from the sector-wide chip volatility and worth tracking on their own.
