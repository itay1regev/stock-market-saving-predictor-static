## Types of fees

| Fee | What it is | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| **Expense ratio** | Annual fund fee, deducted automatically | 0.03%–1.5% |
| **Commission** | Charged per trade by your broker | 0 to a few dollars per trade |
| **Custody / account fee** | For holding securities | 0%–0.2% a year |
| **FX conversion** | Spread when converting ILS to USD | 0.1%–1% per conversion |
| **Management fee on savings** | On pension and study-fund balances and deposits | Varies; negotiable |

## Why small numbers matter

Take 100,000 invested for 30 years at a 7% gross return:

| Annual cost | Ending balance |
|---|---|
| 0.1% | ~739,000 |
| 0.5% | ~661,000 |
| 1.0% | ~574,000 |

The difference between 0.1% and 1% is more than 160,000, money that went to fees instead of compounding for you.

## How to keep costs low

- Choose broad index funds with low expense ratios.
- Compare total cost, not just the headline fee.
- Trade less often.
- Negotiate management fees on your pension and study funds. They are often negotiable. Compare current fees on our [Keren Hishtalmut](#/gemel/hishtalmut) and [Pension](#/gemel/pensia) pages.
