## What happened

- On its Nov 2 earnings call, Apple reported fiscal Q4 2026 revenue of $114.5 billion and net income of $30.7 billion, both beating estimates.
- That capped a record fiscal year: $478.8 billion in annual revenue, up 15% year-over-year.
- AAPL still fell 1.9% to $331.90 on Nov 3 after management guided December-quarter gross margin to 45-46%, below what analysts had modeled.
- The stock has since recovered well beyond that level, closing above $350 for the first time on Nov 9 and setting a fresh record close of $356.27 on Nov 13, pushing Apple's market cap past $5 trillion.

## Why it matters

The margin guidance is the detail that matters more than the headline beat: it's management's own read on near-term cost pressure, and it's the number analysts will be checking against next quarter regardless of where the stock price has since drifted.
