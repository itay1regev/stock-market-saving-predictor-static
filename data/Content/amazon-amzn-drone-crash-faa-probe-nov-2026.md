## What happened

- The FAA is investigating an Amazon delivery drone crash in Michigan near Detroit, confirmed as of Nov 24.
- Earlier in the month, a failed launch of an Amazon "Leo" satellite was cited alongside a new US AI chip export restriction rule as factors behind AMZN falling 3.34% to $257.75 on Nov 16.
- Neither incident is financially material on its own, but together they represent two different Amazon frontier bets -- autonomous delivery and satellite internet -- hitting operational snags in the same stretch.

## Why it matters

Amazon is running several capital-intensive moonshots at once (drones, Kuiper satellites, AI infrastructure). Isolated setbacks are normal at this stage, but it's worth tracking whether they cluster into a pattern or stay one-off.
