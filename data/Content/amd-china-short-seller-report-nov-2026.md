## What happened

- AMD fell 13.25% to $424.39 on Nov 20 after a short-seller report on its China ties deepened a sector-wide AI-chip selloff, then fell another 8.9% on Nov 24 as chips gave back gains from the Nvidia-reinstatement rally.
- On the positive side, AMD is investing $2.5 billion in Anthropic, part of an up-to-$5-billion chip-delivery-linked commitment announced around Nov 10.
- AMD posted record Q3 revenue of $13.4 billion on Nov 3, with Q4 guidance of roughly $17 billion.
- Not on the core watchlist, but AMD is directly entangled in this month's chip-sector story -- the Nvidia CEO saga, the new export-control rule, and the AI capex buildout all move its stock too.

## Why it matters

AMD is a useful cross-check on the Nvidia/TSMC narrative: when the sector-wide story is sentiment-driven (CEO drama, export rules), AMD swings with it; when it's about AMD's own execution (the Anthropic deal, the Q3 beat), the stock has separate, company-specific reasons to move. Worth distinguishing which is which before reacting to any single day's move.
