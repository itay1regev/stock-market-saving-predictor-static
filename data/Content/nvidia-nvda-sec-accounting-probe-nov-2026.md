## What happened

- Reuters reported Nov 19 that the SEC is probing Nvidia's accounting practices; California Attorney General Rob Bonta served the company with an investigative subpoena the same week.
- That overhang helped drive NVDA down 14.0% to $125.53 on Nov 20 -- a fresh 52-week low -- even before the CEO-resignation drama peaked days later.
- Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO on Nov 22-23 triggered a 31%+ single-day rally, but CNBC's Nov 24 follow-up ("Nvidia brought Jensen Huang back. But the investigations aren't over") makes clear the SEC probe, DOJ attention, and congressional scrutiny are all still active.
- None of the legal questions have been resolved -- only the leadership question has.

## Why it matters

A reinstated CEO fixed the governance headline, not the accounting one. Investors cheering the rally should keep an eye on how the SEC and California AG proceedings develop -- that's the overhang that doesn't go away with a board vote.
