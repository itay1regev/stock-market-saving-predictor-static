Michael Burry just un-shorted Nvidia — sort of.

- Sold 75% of his NVDA put options after the SEC-probe-driven selloff.
- Bought long-dated, out-of-the-money NVDA calls as a hedge, keeping some short-dated puts.
- Closed out ALL his semiconductor ETF (SMH) put positions, citing rich option premiums.
- Trimmed Nasdaq-100 put exposure by half; locked in gains on Zoetis calls.
- Biggest longs: MercadoLibre (stake up 20%) and Temple & Webster, each over 10% of the book.

He calls NVDA "a heck of a trade" — but his real conviction is still in beaten-down value, not AI.
